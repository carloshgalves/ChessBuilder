# Training
Personal exercises are generated from selected deeper evaluations (depth ≥10), with a loss of at least five percentage points. Alternative root lines are accepted only if recorded within the score tolerance; unknown legal moves are adjudicated on demand by bounded Stockfish searches; inconclusive results are left ungraded. Personal exercises currently assess the first move; Lichess CSV puzzles assess the full supplied line. The CSV must include PuzzleId, FEN, Moves, Rating and Themes. Lichess puzzle data is CC0. The initial move is the opponent's setup move. Hints and attempts persist. Source PGN and game history remain local.
Superseded items are retired during regeneration, preserving attempt history. Imported puzzle solutions must end on a user move.
