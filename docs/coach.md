# Daily coach
Competence denominators are witnessed opportunities, deduplicated by game/ply/theme and partitioned by phase and speed. Structural hypotheses are excluded. The reported rates are conditional on engine-line witnesses, not exhaustive tactical opportunities. Wilson 95% intervals and a minimum of eight observations guard against overinterpretation. Expected score loss uses WDL where available. The deterministic plan allocates a fixed time budget, prioritizes recurrent weaknesses, due reviews and diversity, and persists session attempts. Novel and repeat practice are separate; changes in game observations are descriptive, not causal estimates of transfer. A fixed provisional review interval is used until the dedicated spaced-repetition module.
